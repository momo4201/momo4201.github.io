About Me

I am a final-year student of Biomedical Engineering working on signal processing and Machine Learning to analyze, model and interpret neural and physiological signals.

I'm interested in EEG and brain computer interfaces, biosensors, instrumentation, neuroprosthetics and the application of AI/ML in healthcare. My current research work covers EEG and ECG based sleep staging and deep learning based lung sound classification for pulmonary disease screening. Currenty, I'm working on the modelling of human attention mechanisms during visual perception.

Outside academic activities, I enjoy reading the random facts about arts, nature, human psychology, science and exploring new ideas. I also love playing table tennis during my free time. I recently founded the IEEE EMBS Student Chapter at NIT Rourkela and currently serve as its Chapter Chair.
